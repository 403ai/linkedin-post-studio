# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want LinkedIn Post Studio to be a welcoming project for people learning, contributing, and building useful writing tools together.

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and patient. Disagreement is welcome when it is focused on the work and expressed with care.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and professional.
- Assume good intent while still naming concrete problems.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.
- Respect different experience levels.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.
- Spam or promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; reject pull requests; or block participants when needed to protect the project community.

If you need to report behavior, open a private security advisory or contact the maintainers through the support path in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
